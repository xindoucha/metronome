## Included Files

The app keeps only the one-shot samples needed by the metronome sounds:

- `kick.wav` from `Sample Drum Kit/Kick.wav`
- `hat-closed.wav` from `Sample Drum Kit/Closed Hat.wav`
- `snare.wav` from `Sample Drum Kit/Sample 1.wav`
- `rim.wav` from `Sample Drum Kit/Sample 2.wav`
